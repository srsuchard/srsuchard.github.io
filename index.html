About

Hi, I'm Samuel, a student developer who likes shipping. I build apps, tools, and hardware that solve problems I run into myself: losing my place in a project, staying focused, proving the work behind an assignment.

My projects range from learning platforms like StudyOS and LearnTrace to LifeOS, an AI-powered personal operating system, and Anchor, an open-source 3D-printed NFC puck that locks distracting apps.

I'm always looking for honest feedback from students, developers, and makers. Email me at samuel.suchard@gmail.com, or find me on GitHub.
